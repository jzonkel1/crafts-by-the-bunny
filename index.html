About the bunny

Hi, I'm Shai. (It's "Sh-AYYY.")

Multi-media artist, mom, wife, and Corpus Christi's official hype-woman. I paint, I bead, I glue feathers to things, and I turn found charms into sun catchers and jewelry. Every piece is one of one, and most of them come with a story or a poem.

This city has shown up for me in a big way, and I try to give it back: talking art with young people through the Arts-for-At-Risk-Youth program, volunteering with the Reflections program through the Corpus Christi Council of PTAs, and setting up at as many local shows as this stay-at-home mom can manage.
