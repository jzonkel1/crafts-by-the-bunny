About the artist

Hi, I'm Shai.Say it like “Sh-AYYY.”

I'm a mixed-media artist in Corpus Christi. A painting starts in acrylic, then it keeps growing: beads, feathers, charms, collage, gold-outlined shapes. You'll find a lot of eyes, suns, oceans and flower-of-life patterns in my work.

My paintings carry real stories about healing, family and dreams. My first true painting, The Biggest Hearts Carry the Heaviest Burdens, came in 2020 and showed me what I was capable of. Prints are how these pieces come home with you.
