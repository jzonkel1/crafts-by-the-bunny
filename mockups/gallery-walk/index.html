V. The artist

Hi, I'm Shai.Say it like Sh-AYYY.

I make loud, devotional, more-is-more paintings: acrylic layered with beads, feathers, charms, collage and gold-lined mosaic shapes. Eyes, suns and oceans keep finding their way in.

Some pieces came from a tarot reading, some from a dream, some from healing my inner child so I could be the mom I wanted to be. The originals are one of a kind. The prints are how they get to come home with you.
