About the artist

Hi, I'm Shai.

Say it like “Sh-AYYY.”

I'm a mixed-media artist in Corpus Christi. I build my paintings out of acrylic, beads, feathers, charms and collage, and a lot of them are about healing: my inner child, the patterns I wanted to break, the people I love.

The originals are one of a kind. Prints are how they get to go home with you. The sun catchers are the small, sparkly side of the studio.
