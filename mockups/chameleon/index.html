About the artist

Hi, I'm Shai.

Say it Sh-AYYY.

I'm a multi-media artist in Corpus Christi, a dedicated mother and wife, and an official hype-woman. I start with acrylic and keep going: beads, feathers, charms, collage, gold-outlined shapes.

My paintings carry dreams, poems, songs and the people I love. Prints let them hang in your home too.

Along the way

  • The Bend Magazine Local's List Winner 2026, Multi-Media Artist
  • Guest on the Corpus Christi Originals Podcast, episode 130
  • Exhibited at the Art Center of Corpus Christi: Pawtrait Studio 2024 and the Meet the Parents show
  • PTA Reflections coordinator and co-host of ArtVenture Culture Fest
  • Arts for At-Risk Youth talk with CC Medical Center, Raps & Apps CC and Texas Cultural Trust, August 2026
  • 5,000+ followers on Instagram and Facebook
