About the artist

Hi, I'm Shai.

Say it Sh-AYYY.

My paintings start with something real: a dream about an owl, a tarot reading, the song my son always asked for, our family dog. Then the layers go on. Acrylic, beads, feathers, charms and collage.

Look closely and you'll find the flower of life tucked into a lot of them. Prints let these pieces go home with more people, and the sun catchers are my little rainbows for your window.
